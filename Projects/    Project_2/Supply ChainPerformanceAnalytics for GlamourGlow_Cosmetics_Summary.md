## 🔍 Key Analytical Findings

1. **Inventory Status by Warehouse**  
   – Skincare SKUs at the Delhi warehouse show the highest stock gaps (> 200 units), while Bangalore and Kolkata remain well-balanced.  
   – Haircare demand is peaking in Mumbai, leading to intermittent back-orders.

2. **Order Fulfillment & Delivery Trends**  
   – Average shipping time across all regions is **5.75 days**, with weekend dispatches routinely slipping past SLA.  
   – Mumbai exhibits the slowest delivery performance (~ 6 days), whereas Chennai and Kolkata average under 6 days.

3. **Supplier Performance Analysis**  
   – **Supplier 1** combines the fastest lead time (~ 12 days), lowest defect rate (~ 1.8 %), and cheapest unit cost—making them the top candidate for increased volume.  
   – Suppliers 3 and 4 are either slow, defect-prone, or high-cost, and should be renegotiated or phased out.

4. **Cost Structure Breakdown**  
   – Logistics/shipping costs account for over **50 %** of total operational expenses, followed by manufacturing and returns.  
   – Promotional campaigns drive sharp spikes in logistics spend, and return volumes climb in Q4.

5. **Shipping Efficiency by Carrier & Route**  
   – **Carrier B** delivers on-time at the lowest average cost (~ ₹ 5.5 per order).  
   – **Carrier A** incurs surcharges on late shipments and **Carrier C** is the most expensive even when on-time.

6. **KPI Snapshot**  
   – **Avg. Shipping Time:** 5.75 days  
   – **Late Shipments:** 17 orders (17 % of total)  
   – **On-Time Rate:** 83 %
